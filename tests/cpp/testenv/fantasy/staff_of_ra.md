# The Staff of Ra

## Overview
The **Staff of Ra** from Raiders of the Lost Ark is the key to finding the Ark of the Covenant.

## Description
- **Purpose**: Divine surveying instrument
- **Function**: Casts shadow pointing to Ark's location
- **Components**: Golden staff with crystal headpiece
- **Timing**: Must be used at specific time and place

## The Headpiece
- **Material**: Bronze medallion with crystal
- **Inscriptions**: Ancient Hebrew text
- **Dimensions**: Determines correct staff height
- **Location**: Found in Marion's tavern in Nepal

## Usage
- **Location**: Map Room in Tanis
- **Time**: Specific hour when sun alignment is correct
- **Result**: Shadow points to exact location of Well of Souls
- **Discovery**: Ark of the Covenant chamber

## Cross-References
- Ark of Covenant (the ultimate prize)
- Elder Wand (another magical staff)
- Holy Grail (Indy's next quest)

The Staff of Ra represents divine guidance in archaeological discovery.
