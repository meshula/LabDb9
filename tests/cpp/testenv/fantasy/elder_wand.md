# The Elder Wand

## Overview
The **Elder Wand** from Harry Potter is the most powerful wand in existence, part of the Deathly Hallows.

## Description
- **Also Known As**: Wand of Destiny, Deathstick
- **Material**: Elder wood with Thestral tail hair core
- **Length**: 15 inches
- **Power**: Unbeatable in duels when properly mastered

## History
- **Creator**: Death himself (according to legend)
- **Previous Owners**: Antioch Peverell, Grindelwald, Dumbledore
- **Current Status**: Returned to Dumbledore's tomb by Harry Potter

## The Deathly Hallows
One of three legendary objects:
- Elder Wand (ultimate magical power)
- Resurrection Stone (power over death)
- Invisibility Cloak (perfect concealment)

## Cross-References
- One Ring (corrupting ultimate power)
- Staff of Ra (magical staff artifact)
- Tesseract (cosmic power source)

The Elder Wand teaches that ultimate power corrupts those who seek it.
