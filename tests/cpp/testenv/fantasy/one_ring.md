# The One Ring

## Overview
The **One Ring** from J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle-earth is perhaps the most famous MacGuffin in fantasy literature.

## Description
- **Creator**: Dark Lord Sauron
- **Material**: Gold, indestructible except in Mount Doom
- **Powers**: Invisibility, domination of other rings, corruption
- **Inscription**: Black Speech in fiery letters

## The Fellowship Quest
- **Bearer**: Frodo Baggins
- **Mission**: Destroy the ring in Mount Doom
- **Opposition**: Sauron, Nazgûl, temptation itself
- **Outcome**: Ring destroyed, Sauron defeated

## Ring Lore
*One Ring to rule them all*
*One Ring to find them*
*One Ring to bring them all*
*And in the darkness bind them*

## Cross-References
- Elder Wand (another ultimate magical artifact)
- Holy Grail (object of epic quest)
- Tesseract (cosmic power object)

The One Ring represents the corrupting nature of absolute power.
