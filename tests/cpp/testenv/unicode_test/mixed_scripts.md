# Mixed Scripts MacGuffins

## Overview
Testing search across multiple writing systems and normalizations.

## Greek (Ελληνικά)
- **Term**: φιλοσοφία (philosophia)
- **Meaning**: Love of wisdom
- **ASCII**: philosophia

## Arabic (العربية)
- **Term**: المعرفة (al-ma'rifa)
- **Meaning**: Knowledge, gnosis
- **Transliteration**: al-marifa

## Chinese (中文)
- **Term**: 智慧 (zhìhuì)
- **Meaning**: Wisdom
- **Pinyin**: zhihui

## Hebrew (עברית)
- **Term**: חכמה (chokhmah)
- **Meaning**: Wisdom
- **Transliteration**: chokhmah

## Japanese (日本語)
- **Term**: 知恵 (chie)
- **Meaning**: Wisdom
- **Romaji**: chie

## Mixed Text Testing
Sentence mixing scripts: The philosopher sought φιλοσοφία through Sanskrit स्वभाव and Hebrew חכמה.

## Normalization Targets
- Case folding across all scripts
- ASCII transliteration where applicable
- Diacritic removal for Latin-based scripts
