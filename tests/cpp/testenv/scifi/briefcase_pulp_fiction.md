# The Briefcase (Pulp Fiction)

## Overview
The **briefcase** from Pulp Fiction (1994) contains something so valuable it drives the entire plot.

## Description
- **Origin**: Belongs to Marsellus Wallace
- **Contents**: Unknown, glows with golden light
- **Combination**: 666
- **Effect**: Mesmerizes anyone who sees inside

## Theories
Popular theories about contents:
- Marsellus Wallace's soul
- Gold bars or diamonds
- Tarantino's MacGuffin (intentionally mysterious)
- The holy light itself

## Cross-References
- Maltese Falcon (another obsession-driving object)
- One Ring (mysterious power over minds)
- Tesseract (glowing artifact of power)

The briefcase represents the ultimate MacGuffin - something valuable precisely because we never know what it contains.
