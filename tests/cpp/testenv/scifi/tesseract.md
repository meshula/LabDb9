# The Tesseract

## Overview
The **Tesseract** appears in multiple sci-fi contexts - Marvel Cinematic Universe and Interstellar.

## Marvel Version
- **True Name**: Space Stone (Infinity Stone)
- **Powers**: Unlimited energy, space manipulation, teleportation
- **Appearance**: Glowing blue cube
- **History**: Hidden on Earth, sought by many

## Interstellar Version
- **Nature**: Five-dimensional construct
- **Purpose**: Communication across time
- **Location**: Inside black hole Gargantua
- **Function**: Allows interaction with past events

## Cross-References
- One Ring (ultimate power object)
- Rabbit's Foot (dangerous artifact)
- Holy Grail (object of cosmic significance)

## Scientific Basis
A tesseract is mathematically a four-dimensional hypercube, representing higher-dimensional space that transcends normal reality.
