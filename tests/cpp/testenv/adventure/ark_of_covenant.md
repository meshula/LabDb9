# The Ark of the Covenant

## Overview
The **Ark of the Covenant** is the ultimate archaeological prize from Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981).

## Description
- **Origin**: Ancient Hebrew artifact
- **Powers**: Divine wrath, supernatural forces
- **Location**: Lost in the desert, found by Indiana Jones
- **Status**: Stored in government warehouse

## Cross-References
Related to other artifacts:
- Staff of Ra (headpiece reveals location)
- Holy Grail (another Indy quest)
- Crystal Skull (later adventure)

The ark contains the original stone tablets of the Ten Commandments.
