# The Holy Grail

## Overview
The **Holy Grail** appears in multiple contexts - from Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade to Monty Python.

## Description
- **Origin**: Cup used by Christ at the Last Supper
- **Powers**: Eternal life, healing
- **Location**: Temple in Petra, guarded by ancient knight
- **Test**: Choose wisely among many cups

## Variants
- **Indy version**: Simple wooden cup
- **Python version**: Source of comedy
- **Arthurian**: Quest object for Knights of Round Table

## Cross-References
- Ark of Covenant (previous Indy adventure)
- Staff of Ra (headpiece artifact)
- One Ring (another powerful artifact)

The grail represents the ultimate spiritual quest.
