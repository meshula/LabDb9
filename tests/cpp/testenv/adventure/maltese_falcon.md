# The Maltese Falcon

## Overview
The **Maltese Falcon** is the iconic MacGuffin from Dashiell Hammett's noir classic (1941).

## Description
- **Origin**: 16th century Knights of Malta
- **Material**: Gold encrusted with jewels, painted black
- **Value**: Priceless historical artifact
- **Status**: The stuff that dreams are made of

## The Hunt
Multiple parties seek the falcon:
- Sam Spade (detective)
- Brigid O'Shaughnessy (femme fatale)
- Kasper Gutman (the Fat Man)
- Joel Cairo (mysterious foreigner)

## Cross-References
- Briefcase from Pulp Fiction (mysterious contents)
- Ark of Covenant (another priceless artifact)
- One Ring (object of obsessive desire)

The falcon represents obsession and betrayal in classic noir tradition.
